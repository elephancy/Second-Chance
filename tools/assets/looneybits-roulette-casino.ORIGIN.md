- Original download: `looneybits-roulette-casino.svg`, SHA-256 `3c36dfaa7e3c915f5e1caba4b958096ba96f963393aed9b33c7fbe264bf9b970`.
- Game export: `looneybits-roulette-wheel-64.png`, SHA-256 `3f2e04d4786d90ea4534f56d11c06b95ac5c51fa3c43c1d0a5a1129f40ffb8b2`.

The original Inkscape SVG was rendered at a 700 by 1000 browser viewport. The wheel was cropped from `(176, 202, 606, 632)`, masked to its circular rim and resized to 64 by 64 with Pillow Lanczos. No part of the earlier Second Chance wheel image was used. `tools/adventure/import_casino_wheel_art.py --check` verifies both pinned sources and both shipped table textures.
