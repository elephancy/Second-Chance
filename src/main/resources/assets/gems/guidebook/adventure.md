# Second Chance

## Quick start

- Craft a **Rumor Atlas** with a compass in the center and paper around it.
- Sneak-use it to pick a place, then use it in the Overworld for a direction and rough distance. Start at The Square if you need a bed or supplies.
- Look at each area's side buildings and the changes between their accounts. The Atlas finds the area, not every door.

Go alone or with friends, and visit places in any order. Your gem works everywhere; death does not erase places you found, and PvP is optional. After your group chooses a story, the Story Council points to the next place.

## Places to explore

At **The Square**, find the market west, guesthouse north, townhall south, and Ragab's games east. Ragab explains each game's rules before you bet; the daily tournament is free.

At **Framefall**, a pale path and blue-glass gateway connect the Atelier and Gallery. Look for clues in the garden and observatory too.

At **Lastlight**, search the market and control room by the brick Exchange. The old clock and newer timetable disagree about the platform.

At **Rehearsal**, explore the prop foundry and costume house beside the Works. Watch where the footlights fall during the Punchline fight.

In **Branch Taiga**, follow the stone markers to the Vault. Its map room and escape tower hint at two ways through.

## After a boss win

Beat a boss to unlock a tool everyone can use at the townhall in The Square. Follow the signs: **Prep** and **Routes** are west; **Tactics** and **Stories** are east. The Story Council, Townhall Book, Meeting Point, and Knowledge Shelf are in the middle aisle. The sections below explain what each place unlocks.

### Framefall: Angle Desk

After the **Painted Verdict**, sneak-use the desk to choose a place. Use it normally to get an Angle Lens. Once you reach that place, stand on clear, solid ground and sneak-use the lens to mark a meeting spot for everyone. Use the lens normally to find it. The marker works within 96 blocks of the place and 48 blocks above or below it; it points the way but does not clear a path.

### Lastlight: Return Board

After the **Last Arrival**, claim a Return Stub. Sneak-use it on solid, open Overworld ground to set a return point for everyone for 60 seconds. Anyone with a stub can return from within 128 blocks and 32 blocks of height. The destination must be loaded, clear and dry. Your stub recharges for 45 seconds after a return. Restoring the Ash Annex in the Nether extends the point to 120 seconds and the range to 256 blocks for everyone.

### Rehearsal: Practice Stage

After the **Unbroken Punchline**, try the harmless Practice Stage. Its gold spotlight follows you, turns pink when it locks, then falls. Step out of the pink circle. The stage resets in ten seconds.

### Branch: Route Desk

After the **Second-Move Solver**, sneak-use the desk to choose one of seven expeditions. Use it normally for a way to a side building. Follow your Atlas to reach the area.

### Redline: Meeting Point

After the **Red Line Between**, claim a Meeting Bell. Sneak-use it on clear, solid ground to set a meeting point for everyone for three minutes. Players within 128 blocks hear the bell; anyone holding one can use it to find the spot. Restoring Ember Bridge in the Nether extends the meeting point to five minutes and the call to 256 blocks.

### Unwritten: Stage Desk

After the **Stray Cadenza**, sneak-use the desk to choose Spotlight, Crosscut or Chorus. Use it normally to check your choice. The next rematch starts with that move, then your choice clears. The rest of the fight still reacts to your party.

### Hushwire: Townhall Book

After anyone clears Hushwire, use the townhall book to call a scout for 30 seconds. You can call another after one minute.

## Other places and their rewards

### Hamed: Inkbound Workshop

Three notes surround Inkbound's old press. Each print has a different use; the paper garden keeps what the town chooses to remember. Finishing the workshop opens the Inkbound Desk for everyone. Its help lasts 40 seconds, then recharges for 90 seconds.

### Khaled: Riverward

Riverward's marked stone faces a floodway with roofed refuges and a workshop beyond. The water warns before it rises; failed crossings can be tried again. Winning opens the Shelter Desk for everyone: 30 seconds of protection, then a 90-second wait.

### Nour: Harbor

The boathouse and foghouse remember a call differently. Their logs and the harbor lamps still carry traces of it. Completing the harbor opens the Harbor Map at the townhall for everyone. Sneak-use it to choose a place; use it to share the way there for 30 seconds. It recharges in 90 seconds.

Find a Harbor Buoy in harbor chests or craft one with copper, glass and string. Use it beside open water to make a light friends can follow for 30 seconds.

### Fam: Forum Glade

The Story Hall and Open School tell different sides of Fam's forum. The board beside Fam keeps the town's account. Friends can explore together or apart. Finishing it opens the Welcome Board for everyone.

### Adl: Survey Highlands

The high sightpost and chart shelter see Adl's valley from different angles. Their markings reach across the Survey House. Finishing the survey opens the Atlas Pin board for everyone. Claim a pin; sneak-use it in the Overworld to set or clear a shared meeting point. Use it to find that point.

### Othman: Library

The public catalog and private study hold two useful but different leads. At the main hall desks, choose which one the town should know. Either choice keeps progress. Everyone can read the shared lead at the townhall Knowledge Shelf; the other stays in the library.

### Yehia: Storybook Vale

The Truth Garden and Prop House tell the same journey differently. The Story Lodge holds both accounts, and the garden remembers its own. A wrong turn restarts the tale without penalty. Finishing lets everyone claim a Story Thread at the townhall. Use it near a landmark to find an unopened stash.

### Omar: Lost Quarter

An Exchange Court receipt and an upstairs Memory Loft notebook disagree with Omar's missing shelf labels. A wrong guess in the lane costs nothing. Afterward, Lost-and-Found can point toward a place you found but have not finished.

After the puzzle, claim a Recovery Slip. It remembers your last death and points roughly toward it when nearby. You can give it to a friend. Near the spot, it can point out a loose item, but cannot prove who owns it.

### Soryal: Repair Yard

The scrap bridge, upstairs loft and yard floor carry marks from the same old repair. A wrong patch resets the pattern without using materials. Finishing it opens the Repair Bench for everyone. Hold a worn tool and bring one iron and one copper ingot to restore up to a quarter of its durability.

### Ali: Splitstone Heights

The high map and older gate rubbing disagree about a traveled arch. Worn stone still holds its outline. Finishing Splitstone opens the Shortcut Board. Claim chalk; sneak-use it at two safe Overworld spots, five to 128 blocks apart and within 32 blocks of height. Use it near either mark to travel between them. Sneak-use again to start a new pair.

## Choose the world's story

After two boss identities are revealed, the townhall Story Council offers **Remember**, **Repair** or **Release**. Sneak-use it to see or change your vote. When everyone within 16 blocks has voted, use the council twice within 30 seconds to confirm the majority choice. A tie waits for another vote. The whole server follows one route and shares chapter progress. The council points to the next place.

### Remember

Remember follows Hamed's prints, Othman's two records, and Yehia's garden tale. Each place holds an account that changed with its telling. A mistaken mark at Yehia's garden restarts only that attempt.

### Repair

Repair returns to Soryal's cracked fittings, Khaled's flood refuges, and Redline's shelters. Their damage and old work leave traces of what happened. Completed repairs stay saved, even if a timed crossing fails.

### Release

Release follows Nour's harbor call, Ali's lost passage, and Fam's shared note. Listen for the different voices in each place. Fam's note can be handed to a friend; a lost note can be replaced at the board. Timed attempts can be restarted.

## Beyond the Overworld

**Ash Annex, Ember Bridge, and End Source are part of the main story.** Your
Atlas points to them when you are ready.

- **Ash Annex:** Beat the Last Arrival at Lastlight. Enter the Nether through a
  normal portal, select the Ash Annex Atlas page, and use it there to find a
  depot in the Nether wastes. Its station book, control room and platform each
  preserve part of an interrupted departure. Restoring the depot improves the
  townhall Return Stub for everyone.
- **Ember Bridge:** Beat Redline's guardian. In the Nether, use the Ember
  Bridge Atlas page to search a warped forest. The two shelters show their
  missing masonry and metal. The watch line, center and far mark trace a
  defended crossing. This improves
  the townhall Meeting Bell for everyone.
- **End Source:** Restore both Nether routes, recover all seven identities,
  and finish your chosen story's three tasks. Use an End gateway to
  reach the outer islands; the End Source Atlas page points the way there.
  The center lens responds to the story your world chose. The side galleries
  hold what it needs. The return arch works before and after the chapter;
  sneak-use any surviving source mark if the arch is blocked.

The Atlas gives a direction only while you are **in the right dimension**.
Use an Annex gate or, after it opens, sneak-use its Return board mark to go
home. At Ember Bridge, use its gate or sneak-use the east shelter mark. Each
return checks for a safe Overworld landing. If no site appears nearby, travel
farther into new terrain in that dimension and search again; already explored
chunks do not grow a new building.

## The last chapter

After your three route leads, all seven boss identities, and the open distant
chapters, return to the center stage at Unwritten Court. Its side rooms are the
mask archive and steward's annex. Friends can split up. If interrupted,
restart at the center stage. The End Source changes how your chosen story begins and
gives it more time here.

Pale stage ribs and paired mask crests mark the Court and side rooms. The open south arch leads back to the center stage.

The side rooms hold evidence the Court left out. The center stage begins and closes each telling. If a timed attempt expires, delivered materials and earlier chapters stay saved.

The center tile changes when you finish. The Story Council becomes an archive, relief depot or crossroads desk for your world. Use it once each Minecraft day for a travel kit; sneak-use it to read the ending. Boss rematches stay available.

## Fights and breaks

Watch colors and listen for warnings. Some attacks follow you, then lock onto a spot. Move or take cover after the lock; strike during the pause. At Framefall, blue follows you and orange marks the locked shot. You can leave a hard fight and return later. After a win, speak to the boss for a rematch. Players who help fight or stay nearby for three seconds receive their own first-win story item and supplies. Rematches give supplies again; the last hit does not decide rewards.

The Square has guesthouse beds and market supplies. Ragab's casino shows each game's rules and emerald stake. Losing a wager cannot cost your gem, hearts or adventure progress.

Ragab's **free daily tournament** features three solo games each Minecraft day, plus an optional two-player Signal Table. Win three different featured games to fill the shared Tournament Board. One player can win all three solo games, or friends can split the wins. A Signal Table win can replace one solo win. Free rounds cost no emeralds. Your first completed day unlocks Ragab's shared reward. After each completed day, every player can claim two bread and four torches from the board once that day.
