# Go off the main road

Three small adventures appear beyond the friend districts. Use the Rumor Atlas
to search for Lost Trail, Star Cipher, or Skywatch Lookout.

At Lost Trail, the stone ribbon remembers a road that is gone. Its bends and
end markers tell the route. At Star Cipher, the inscription and three sky
symbols share an order; the seal responds when they agree. At Skywatch, a
spyglass fits the high dial. Its rim and view of the horizon provide the bearing.

The first time you finish each place, you earn supplies. Friends can earn their own too.
If your inventory is full, make space and collect them when you return.

The Ninefold Maze is a much longer journey in open plains and savanna. The
Atlas can point you there. Bring light and food. Each floor has its own visual
rule and a blue checkpoint to save your progress. Copper reset lamps return
you to your last safe point without erasing solved seals. Each friend who
solves the maze can earn their own first reward.

After you finish one of these places, sneak-use its marker for 90 seconds of
easier travel and better sight. You can use it again after ten minutes.
