# The Undercard

The Undercard is an optional fight club below a dryland quarry. Conflicting stories name different winners of the same match. Its changing tally wall invites visitors to settle the tale with three clean bells. It does not gate a boss, gem, townhall service or ending.

## Visit and bouts

A ruined weigh-house hides a lit ladder down an enclosed quarry throat to a brass ring, unequal spectator tiers, kit bays and a service exit. Visitors can leave by the ladder without winning. The venue is kept bright enough for safe play, isolated from Deep Dark and ancient-city hazards, and protected against ordinary hostile spawns during a bout.

Use the ring bell for three solo sparring rounds. The clockwork construct moves between lanes; avoid its gold sweep, then counter during the blue window. A missed counter repeats that round without damage or permanent loss. Hits cannot kill it, drop loot or grant gem energy.

Sneak-use the bell to invite another player. A second player accepts within 30 seconds. The first to land three clean, nonlethal punches wins. Damage and knockback are cancelled for the participants; spectators may watch. Leaving, dying, disconnecting or timing out ends a bout without a result. Restart clears transient bouts. A winner receives one Winner's Brass per site, with delivery deferred if their inventory is full; rematches do not pay again.
