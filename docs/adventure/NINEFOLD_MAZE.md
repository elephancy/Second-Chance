# The Ninefold Maze

The maze is a rare copper and dark-stone landmark in plains, sunflower plains,
and savannas. Its gateway stands beneath a roofline of nine crowns. Bring food,
light, and a friend if you like; every puzzle can also be finished alone.

Use the glowing entrance stone to begin. The first floor's sky glyphs follow
the passage's change from day into night. The second floor asks you to read
the near and far reflections. On the top floor, three witnesses hold the last
seal. Save at each blue checkpoint after a floor answers; the final door needs
all three seals and checkpoints.

Side passages contain supply caches and shortcuts, but some hold echo,
dart, snare, or false-door traps. A copper reset stone takes you back to your
last safe point without erasing solved seals. Players who complete the route
near the final door together receive their own first-clear field supplies.
An inventory with no room can claim those supplies later.
