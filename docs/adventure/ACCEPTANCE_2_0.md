# Second Chance 2.0 final play checklist

This is the single integrated player acceptance pass for the **Minecraft 26.3** fresh-world release. Record the hashes of the exact 2.0 jars and dependencies before play. Any change to a jar after the pass requires revisiting the affected observations. Historical trial worlds are reference evidence only.

## Prepare

- Start a new survival world with an ordinary packaged client. Record the seed, client and server versions, installed mod hashes, player IDs, and start time. Keep a copy of the untouched world before the first visit.
- Use a resource-capped dedicated server for the shared portion. Keep the local PC to one rendered Minecraft client at a time if memory is low. Use an authenticated server for the remote join check.
- Creative/admin commands may stage supplies, inspect site locations and diagnose failures. Mark every such action in the notes. A command-placed structure, forced victory or teleport does not count as natural discovery, travel or challenge completion.

## Play

- Follow the guidebook's opening loop, then find and complete all 18 people's areas. Check each arrival, dialogue, clue, objective, reward and unlocked townhall service. Play all seven bosses, a rematch, the Four Roads Estate and its center exit, Ninefold Maze, Undercard, Sunken Relay, Underarchive and other encountered exploration sites.
- Reach Ash Annex and Ember Bridge in the Nether and End Source in the End through ordinary dimension travel. Check that paths, lighting, portals and return routes remain usable. Finish the Court and save the pre-choice world. From separate copies of that stopped save, play Remember, Repair and Release to their different outcomes. Reopen each ending after restart.
- Join a second player late, check shared unlocks and individual rewards, then repeat a townhall use and one rematch after server restart. Verify that both players can continue without the first player's commands or client.
- Open the guidebook at wide and compact sizes. Click Previous and Next across short and long chapters, then check search, close/reopen and settings. Play every casino game, including an idle or interrupted round, a win, a loss and a next-day return. Inspect the actual game window and listen to important sound cues while playing.

## Record the result

For each area or system, record what the player did, whether it worked, the relevant server/client log span, the save/restart result and any Minecraft-window-only screenshot that helps explain a visual result. Do not capture the desktop or unrelated apps. Continuous recording is outside this checklist. Keep failed observations as failures; fix them in a coherent batch and replay the affected part on the final jars.

A package build, script, source manifest or screenshot of a static build cannot by itself pass gameplay, sound, two-player or ending acceptance. The final status should state each observed pass, remaining failure and unobserved outcome separately.
