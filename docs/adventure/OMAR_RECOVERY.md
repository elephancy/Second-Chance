# Recovery Slip

Solve the missing-object puzzle in Omar's Lost Wadi, then use Lost-and-Found at
the townhall to claim a Recovery Slip. It belongs to you, but you can hand it
to a friend.

After you die, use the slip in the same dimension to follow a rough direction
back to your last fall. A friend holding your slip can follow it too. Once the
holder is close, using it searches nearby loaded ground and briefly marks a
loose item. Check the item before assuming it is yours.

The slip cannot bring items back, keep them from despawning, or search from
more than 1,024 blocks away. It keeps only the latest fall for each player.
